# 🥤 Drinks Sales Data Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project involves an end-to-end commercial analysis of a regional beverage portfolio. The objective was to evaluate sales performance across multiple brands, flavours, and European markets to identify high-value revenue drivers and areas for strategic improvement. This project forms part of the NCFE Level 2 Data Analysis certification.

## 2. Methodology & Data Standardisation
Before analysis, the raw dataset underwent rigorous quality assurance to ensure data integrity. Key cleaning processes included:

* **Categorical Standardisation:** Corrected brand naming inconsistencies (e.g., transforming 'Coke' to 'Cola' and 'Fruity' to 'Fruit' using array formulas).
* **Data Enrichment:** Expanded truncated abbreviations into full nomenclature (e.g., 'Che' to 'Cherry', 'Lem' to 'Lemon') and standardised regional naming conventions ('United Kingdom' to 'UK').
* **Type Casting:** Converted core metrics (Units Sold, Unit Cost) from text strings to calculable numerical formats to allow for accurate aggregation.

## 4. Key Commercial Insights 

**1. Cola is the Market Leader**
The 'Cola' brand acts as the portfolio's primary volume driver, securing 52.7% of all units sold and 52.2% of total revenue. This indicates a strong, established consumer preference, marking it as the “low-risk” anchor for the business.

**2. Spain & Ireland Drive Revenue**
Spain (£18,510) and Ireland (£17,606) are the top two markets, contributing 39% of total revenue despite representing only 33% of the regional footprint. These markets function as the growth engines of the business.

**3. Cherry is the Hero Product**
Cherry leads all individual flavours with 9,302 units sold, generating £13.4k in revenue. Conversely, Vanilla demonstrates the lowest customer engagement across the regions.

**4. Price Elasticity Exists**
'Normal Cola' in Spain achieved the highest single-product revenue (£3,734) despite carrying a premium price point of £1.92 (well above the £1.52 portfolio average). This proves that customers are willing to pay higher margins for the right product in the right market.

## 5. Strategic Recommendations
1.  **Reallocate Marketing Spend:** Shift promotional resources and budget away from the underperforming Vanilla flavour and redirect them toward capitalising on the existing momentum of the Cherry product line.
2.  **Scale the Blueprint:** Utilise the high-yield strategies currently operating in Spain and Ireland as a blueprint to overhaul operations and scale underperforming regions, such as Italy.
